Advisory Services

Full-Lifecycle Advisory

From the first site visit to final closeout — independent expertise at every stage where capital is at risk.

Development Advisory

Guiding owners, investors, and developers through every stage — from initial concept through entitlement, financing, construction, and stabilization. Site feasibility, development strategy, entitlement planning, financial modeling, and risk assessment.

Owner's Representation

Independent oversight of the entire development and construction process on behalf of the owner. Acting as the owner's advocate — managing architect, contractor, and consultant teams, leading OAC meetings, reviewing pay applications and change orders.

Preconstruction Advisory

Planning, cost certainty, and constructability before construction begins. Preliminary budgeting, value engineering, constructability review, procurement strategy, bid management, and risk mitigation — reducing construction costs by 5–15% and limiting change orders.

Construction Advisory

Independent technical oversight during construction to ensure work is performed properly and the project stays on track. Progress monitoring, schedule compliance, budget tracking, change order analysis, and contractor performance monitoring.

Acquisition & Development Due Diligence

Thorough evaluation before acquiring land or an existing property. Site feasibility, development potential, construction cost projections, zoning analysis, CapEx analysis, and development timeline assessment — helping clients avoid costly mistakes.

Capital Project Oversight

Strategic supervision of large capital investments — project governance, budget monitoring, schedule tracking, contractor oversight, and progress reporting to ownership and investors. Particularly valuable for institutional owners with multiple projects.
